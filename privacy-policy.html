Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide when placing an order, including your name, email address, shipping address, and photos you submit for printing. We do not collect payment information directly — all payments are processed securely through third-party payment processors.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to process and fulfill your orders, communicate with you about your order status, and send occasional promotional emails if you have opted in. We never sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Your Photos

Photos you submit are used exclusively for printing your order. We do not share, sell, or use your photos for any other purpose. Photos are securely stored and deleted after your order is fulfilled unless you request otherwise.

4. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to improve your browsing experience. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

5. Third Party Services

We may use third-party services such as shipping carriers and payment processors. These services have their own privacy policies and we recommend reviewing them.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about our privacy policy, please contact us at contact.thestickypixels@gmail.com
