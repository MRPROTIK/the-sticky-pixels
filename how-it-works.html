Photo Requirements

To ensure the best quality magnets, please follow these guidelines

✅ Accepted Formats

  • ✓ JPG / JPEG
  • ✓ PNG
  • ✓ PDF
  • ✓ Heic (iPhone photos)

📐 Recommended Resolution

  • ✓ Minimum: 300 DPI
  • ✓ Ideal: 600 DPI or higher
  • ✓ File size: Under 25MB
  • ✓ Color mode: RGB

💡 Best Photo Tips

  • ✓ Good lighting makes colors pop
  • ✓ Avoid blurry or pixelated images
  • ✓ Portraits work best centered
  • ✓ Landscape photos great for 5x7

Common Questions

Can I use a screenshot from my phone?

▼

Yes! Screenshots work fine as long as they are clear and not pixelated. The higher the resolution the better.

What if I don't like how my magnet looks?

▼

We offer 100% satisfaction guarantee. If you're not happy, contact us and we'll reprint or refund your order.

Can I order multiple different photos?

▼

Absolutely! You can order multiple magnets with different photos. Just include all photos when you place your order.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes! We ship worldwide. International shipping times may vary by location.
