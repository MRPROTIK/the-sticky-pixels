3D Products FAQ

What colors are available?

▼

We currently offer Red, Black, White, Blue and Pink. Custom colors may be available on request — contact us for details.

How long does printing take?

▼

Each frame is printed to order. Production takes 2-3 days, plus 3-5 days for shipping.

Can I request a custom design?

▼

Absolutely! Contact us at contact.thestickypixels@gmail.com with your idea and we'll discuss options.
