📋 Ordering Tip — Read Before You Order

Before placing your order, measure your photo magnet in millimetres and add 0.5–1mm to that measurement when selecting your frame size.

Why? 3D printing naturally expands filament slightly. Combined with minor printing irregularities and slight roughness from support removal, exact-fit sizing can make your magnets too snug. This small allowance ensures a smooth, secure fit every time.

📐 Magnet Size Guide

  • 🔹 ENTER brand: 50.5 × 50.5 mm → order 50.5mm frame
  • 🔹 TECRE brand: 54 × 54 mm → order 54mm frame
  • 🔹 Memory Mag: 64.5 × 64.5 mm → order 64.5mm frame
  • 🔹 FMCraft / Aliexpress: 64.5 × 64.5 mm → order 64.5mm frame
  • 🔹 Other brands: Measure your magnet + add 0.5–1mm
