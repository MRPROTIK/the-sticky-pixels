OUR STORY

We Started With a Simple Idea

Welcome to The Sticky Pixels — where your favorite moments become magnets you can hold onto forever.

We started with a simple idea: photos shouldn't just live on your phone. They deserve to be seen, touched, and shared every single day. So we created The Sticky Pixels to turn your favorite memories into something tangible and beautiful.

Every magnet we print is handled personally. We use UV-resistant ink and durable materials so your colors stay vivid for 5+ years with daily use. And we stand behind our work — if you're not happy, we'll remake it.

We obsess over the details so you don't have to. Sizing tolerance for 3D frames, metal disc recommendations, photo quality guidance — the little things that make the difference between "nice" and "absolutely perfect."

We're a small team passionate about helping people preserve what matters most. Thank you for trusting us with your memories!
