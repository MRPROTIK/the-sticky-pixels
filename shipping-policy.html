Shipping Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Processing Time

All orders are custom made to order. Please allow 3–5 business days for production before your order ships. During peak seasons or holidays, processing times may be slightly longer. You will receive a confirmation email once your order has shipped.

2. Shipping Rates

  • Free shipping on all orders $35 and over
  • Standard shipping fee applies to orders under $35
  • Expedited shipping available — contact us at contact.thestickypixels@gmail.com for rates
  • International shipping rates vary by destination — contact us for a quote

3. Domestic Shipping (USA)

We ship to all 50 US states. Standard domestic shipping typically takes 3–7 business days after production is complete. Expedited options are available upon request.

4. International Shipping

We ship worldwide! International shipping times vary by destination and typically take 7–21 business days after production. Please note that international orders may be subject to customs duties and taxes which are the responsibility of the recipient. We are not responsible for delays caused by customs.

5. Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you will receive a tracking number via email. You can use this number to track your package through the carrier's website.

6. Shipping Carriers

We use trusted carriers including USPS, UPS and FedEx for domestic orders. International orders are shipped via USPS International or other reliable carriers depending on destination.

7. Lost or Damaged Packages

If your package arrives damaged or is lost in transit, please contact us immediately at contact.thestickypixels@gmail.com with your order number and photos of any damage. We will work with you to resolve the issue as quickly as possible.

8. Wrong Address

Please ensure your shipping address is correct when placing your order. We are not responsible for orders shipped to incorrect addresses provided by the customer. If you notice an error in your shipping address, contact us immediately before production begins.

9. Contact Us

For any shipping questions, contact us at contact.thestickypixels@gmail.com or visit our Contact page.
