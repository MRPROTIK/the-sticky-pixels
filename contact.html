Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How long does shipping take?

▼

Magnets ship within 1–3 business days. 3D Frames ship within 3–5 business days after production is complete.

Can I order in bulk?

▼

Absolutely! We offer bulk pricing for events, weddings, corporate gifts and more. Use the contact form above or visit our Bulk Orders page.

What file format should I use for my photo?

▼

We accept JPG, PNG and PDF files. For best results, use a high-resolution photo (at least 300 DPI).
