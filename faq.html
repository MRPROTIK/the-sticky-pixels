Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about The Sticky Pixels

🛍️ Orders & Customization

How do I place an order?

▼

Simply browse our products, choose your size and style, then send us your photo via email at contact.thestickypixels@gmail.com or use our contact form. We'll confirm your order and get started right away!

What photo formats do you accept?

▼

We accept JPG, JPEG, PNG, PDF and HEIC (iPhone) files. For best results, use a high-resolution image of at least 300 DPI.

Can I order multiple different photos in one order?

▼

Absolutely! You can include multiple photos in a single order. Just send all your photos when placing your order and let us know which product each photo is for.

Can I see a preview before you print?

▼

For standard orders we proceed directly to printing. For bulk or custom orders, we can provide a digital proof upon request.

Do you offer custom sizes?

▼

Our standard sizes are 2.5x2.5, 2.5x3.5, 3.25x4 and 5x7 inches. Contact us for custom sizing requests.

Magnets ship within 1–3 business days. 3D Frames ship within 3–5 business days after production is complete. We ship within Canada via Canada Post.

Do you ship internationally?

▼

We currently ship within Canada only via Canada Post. If you are located outside Canada please contact us at contact.thestickypixels@gmail.com to discuss options.

How much does shipping cost?

▼

Shipping rates are calculated based on your location within Canada. Contact us for specific shipping rate inquiries.

⭐ Quality & Returns

What if I'm not happy with my order?

▼

We understand — sometimes colors look different on screen than in print, or a photo doesn't come out quite as expected. That's not your fault and we can fix it. We'll remake your order for free. You just cover the return shipping, which is usually just a few dollars. Reach out within 7 days of receiving your order and we'll make it right. Our goal is for you to absolutely love your magnets.

What quality materials do you use?

▼

We use premium quality magnetic material and high-resolution ink printing to ensure your photos look vivid and sharp. Our magnets are durable and long-lasting.

Will the colors look exactly like my photo?

▼

Not exactly — but very close. Here's why: your screen uses light to display colors while our printer uses ink, so they don't always match perfectly. What helps: a high-resolution photo (600+ DPI) gives better color accuracy. We color-calibrate regularly and use quality ink so your colors will be vivid and true to your original photo. Not happy with the colors when you receive it? We'll remake it.

How long will my magnets last?

▼

Our magnets are built to last 5+ years with daily use. We use UV-resistant ink so colors stay vivid even on sunny fridges — they won't fade over time. The magnetic backing is durable and won't weaken either. We have customers with the same magnets on their fridges for years with no quality loss. That's what we aim for with every order.

📐 3D Products — Sizing & Ordering

Before I order a 3D frame, what do I need to know?

▼

Measure your photo magnet in millimetres and add 0.5–1mm to that measurement when selecting your frame size. Why? 3D printing naturally expands filament slightly during printing. Combined with minor irregularities and slight roughness from support removal, exact-fit sizing can make your magnets too snug. This small allowance ensures a smooth, secure fit every time. Example: if your magnet is 53mm, order a 54mm frame.

Do you have a sizing guide for different magnet brands?

▼

Yes! ENTER brand: 50.5mm. TECRE brand: 54mm. Memory Mag: 64.5mm. FMCraft: 64.5mm. Aliexpress (China): 64.5mm. If your brand isn't listed, measure your magnet and add 0.5–1mm.

What metal discs do you recommend?

▼

We use thin silver round iron discs — they're safer than large flat discs, especially in homes with kids and pets. They're durable, long-lasting and widely available on Amazon at low cost.

What if my magnet doesn't fit after I receive the frame?

▼

Reach out immediately at contact.thestickypixels@gmail.com. We can adjust the design and reprint at a small fee, or remake your order with corrected sizing at no additional charge. We want the fit to be perfect.

🖨️ 3D Products

What colors are available for 3D frames?

▼

We currently offer Red, Black, White, Blue and Pink. Custom colors may be available — contact us for details.

Can I request a custom 3D design?

▼

Yes! We love custom projects. Contact us with your idea and we'll discuss what's possible and provide a quote.

What are STL files and how do I use them?

▼

STL files are 3D printing files that you can use with your own 3D printer to print the frames at home. They're compatible with most FDM printers including Creality, Prusa, Bambu Lab and Anycubic.

How long does 3D printing take?
